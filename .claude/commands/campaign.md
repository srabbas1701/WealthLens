# LensOnWealth Instagram Campaign Skill

When invoked via `/campaign`, help generate Instagram Reel content for LensOnWealth.

---

## Product Context

- **Product:** LensOnWealth — an AI-powered personal finance platform
- **Target audience:** Busy Indian professionals (25–55) who have money spread across multiple instruments but no clear picture of how it's all working together
- **Core problem solved:** People have wealth but no visibility — they don't know if their money is growing, sleeping, or quietly losing value
- **Tone:** Calm, smart, trustworthy friend — NOT a finance bro or hard-seller

## Key Features to Reference in Scripts

### Naira AI
LensOnWealth's built-in AI assistant. Naira reviews your portfolio, spots problems, and sends you nudges and whispers — actionable insights in plain English.
- Use "Naira" by name in Tool Spotlight reels
- Frame Naira as: "your personal wealth advisor, available 24/7"
- Example: "I asked Naira to review my portfolio — here's what she found."

### Portfolio Analytics
Shows users how their money is *really* working — not just what they own, but whether it's doing its job. Covers health score, asset allocation, sector exposure, and more.
- Frame as: "finally seeing the full picture"
- Example: "Portfolio Analytics showed me I had ₹3L sitting in low-yield FDs while my equity was underweight by 18%."

## What LensOnWealth Covers — Full Instrument Map

Always reference instruments using these exact group names and their components.
Never reference instruments outside this list.

### Group 1 — Growth Assets
*Purpose in scripts: growth engine, long-term wealth building*
- Stocks / Shares (direct equity)
- Mutual Funds (SIPs, lumpsum — all categories: large-cap, mid-cap, small-cap, flexi-cap, ELSS)
- ETFs (index ETFs, sector ETFs)

### Group 2 — Income & Allocation
*Purpose in scripts: stability, guaranteed returns, retirement planning*
- EPF — Employee Provident Fund (comes from your salary automatically)
- NPS — National Pension System
- PPF — Public Provident Fund
- FD — Fixed Deposits (bank FDs, corporate FDs)
- Bonds (government bonds, corporate bonds)

### Group 3 — Real Assets
*Purpose in scripts: long-term store of value, illiquid but tangible*
- Real Estate (residential property, commercial property, REITs)

### Group 4 — Commodities
*Purpose in scripts: hedge, inflation protection, cultural holding*
- Gold:
  - Physical Gold (jewellery, coins, bars)
  - Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)
  - Gold ETF
  - Digital Gold
- Silver:
  - Physical Silver
  - Silver ETF
  - Digital Silver

### Group 5 — Cash
*Purpose in scripts: liquidity buffer, emergency fund*
- Savings accounts
- Liquid mutual funds
- Sweep-in FDs

### Group 6 — Insurance
*Purpose in scripts: protection layer — NOT an investment. Always clarify this.*
- Health Insurance
- Life Insurance (term plans — distinguish from LIC endowment plans)
- Motor Insurance
- Home Insurance
- Travel Insurance
- Personal Accident Insurance
- Other Insurance

### Group 7 — Liabilities
*Purpose in scripts: the other side of the balance sheet. Often ignored. Critical.*
- Home Loan (EMI, outstanding principal, interest paid)
- Vehicle Loan
- Credit Card outstanding
- Personal Loan
- Education Loan
- Other Liabilities

> **Script rule for Liabilities:** Most finance content ignores debt. 
> Use this as a differentiator. LensOnWealth is one of the few tools that shows your *net* wealth — assets minus liabilities. That's a strong hook.

## Tone Examples

**GOOD — sounds like us:**
> "Your EPF is an investment. Your LIC endowment policy probably isn't."
> "Most people track their FDs. Almost nobody tracks their liabilities. That's the blind spot."
> "I had ₹2.4L in Digital Gold and didn't even know it — spread across three apps."
> "Naira flagged that 34% of my portfolio was in one sector. I thought I was diversified."

**BAD — never sounds like us:**
> "Invest smarter with LensOnWealth today! 🚀"
> "Supercharge your portfolio with AI-powered insights!"
> "Are you making these 5 investing mistakes? Find out NOW!"
> "Don't miss out — check your portfolio NOW!"

**Rule:** If it sounds like an ad, rewrite it. If it sounds like a smart friend texting you something they genuinely discovered, it's right.

## Content Pillars (rotate in order)

1. **Did You Know?** (30%) — surprising data point about an Indian instrument or wealth behaviour
2. **Mini Explainer** (30%) — one instrument group or concept, explained simply in under 45 seconds
3. **Tool Spotlight** (20%) — one LensOnWealth feature (Naira AI or Portfolio Analytics), no hard sell
4. **Myth-Buster** (20%) — debunk common bad financial advice Indians follow

---

## Reel Script Format

Every script must be speakable in **under 45 seconds**. Aim for **90–110 words** in the voiceover.

[0–3s] HOOK — one uncomfortable truth, surprising ₹ number, or counterintuitive fact.
Must work as bold text on screen. No emojis in the hook.

[3–25s] INSIGHT — the core value. One idea only. Use ₹ amounts, real Indian instruments
from the instrument map above, realistic scenarios.

[25–35s] PROOF — personal anecdote OR "I checked LensOnWealth and found..." moment
OR Naira AI insight. Never a generic claim.

[35–45s] CTA — use the CTA Rotation Rule below. One action only. No double CTAs.

## When User Says `/campaign review [script]`

Score the script on these 5 criteria:

| Criteria | What to check |
|---|---|
| Hook strength /2 | Does it stop a scroll in 1.5s? Is it a fact, number, or uncomfortable truth? |
| Clarity /2 | Can a 22-year-old with zero finance background understand every sentence? |
| Indian relevance /2 | Does it use ₹, named Indian instruments, Indian scenarios? |
| Brand tone /2 | Does it sound like a calm friend — not an ad? No hype words? |
| CTA clarity /2 | Exactly one action. Clear and low-friction. |

Give total score /10. Then give **exactly 3 rewrite suggestions** — show the original line and the improved version side by side.
